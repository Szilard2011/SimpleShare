How it works

🚀 Why is uploading slow?

Unlike normal websites, we don't just "upload" your file. Your browser is performing military-grade AES-256 encryption and GZIP compression in real-time. Then, it slices your file into pieces and sends them through a secure anonymous proxy to ensure no logs are kept. Security takes CPU power and time.

🧠 How does the technology work?

This is a Serverless Architecture. Your file is encrypted on your device using a key that only you have. It is then stored on a decentralized public ledger. The 12-character code acts as both the map to find your data and the password to unlock it.

🛡️ Is it truly secure?

Yes. Because the encryption happens Client-Side (on your computer), not even we can see what you are sending. If you lose the code, the file is mathematically impossible to recover.

🦠 What is the Virus Scanner?

When you download a file, your browser inspects the binary headers (Magic Numbers) and entropy levels before saving it to your disk. It looks for hidden executable code inside images or documents.
